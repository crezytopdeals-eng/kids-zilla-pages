Privacy Policy

Critters Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Clumsy Critters Zoo channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It posts the owner's own animated videos to those accounts.

The app asks for permission to publish videos to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and to upload to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect, store, sell or share any personal data from any other person. No other users use this app.

The access tokens are stored privately by the owner and are used only to publish videos. They are never shared.

Data deletion: the app holds no data about anyone other than its owner. To ask for any data to be removed, or to remove the app's access, email crezytopdeals@gmail.com or remove "Critters Uploader" from your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.
